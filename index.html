The 1-hour trial

The trial is a full training hour, not a sales meeting. Come as you are. Aron will use the time to understand your starting point and work with you.

01

Intake review

Your goals, training history, injuries, and schedule, so the session is relevant from the first minute.

02

Movement and baseline

A practical look at how you squat, push, pull, and move, scaled to your current experience.

03

A working session

Coaching and training you can feel — technique, effort, and a first taste of the work ahead.

04

A clear next step

Honest advice on what a plan should include if you choose to continue, based on your goal and your week.

Coaching that fits the person in front of him.

Every client starts in a different place. Aron trains people who want to lose weight, build muscle, or move with more strength and confidence in daily life. The work is straightforward: sound technique, a plan you can keep, and progress you can measure.
