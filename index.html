About

Coaching that fits the person in front of him.

Every client starts in a different place. Aron trains people who want to lose weight, build muscle, or move with more strength and confidence in daily life.

The work is straightforward: sound technique, a plan you can keep, and progress you can measure. Sessions are one-to-one, in person or online, and shaped around your goal, your experience, and your week.

The trial

How the 1-hour trial works

The trial is a full training hour, not a sales meeting. Three short steps get you there.

  1. 01

    Request a trial

    Fill in a short two-step intake. It takes a few minutes and tells Aron what he needs before you meet.

  2. 02

    Aron prepares the hour

    Your answers shape the session — goals, experience, injuries, and when you can train.

  3. 03

    Train for one hour

    A practical look at how you move, a working session, and honest advice on what should come next.
